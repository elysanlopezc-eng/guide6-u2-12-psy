Core Concept 1

Structured vs. Unstructured

Structured: Uses predefined coding sheets and checklists for quantifiable frequency counts.
Unstructured: Open field notes capturing holistic consumer behavior in natural settings.

Method Versatility 96%
Core Concept 2

Observation Checklists

Standardized instruments listing specific operationalized actions (e.g., touching display, reading label, checking price tag) to record in real-time.

Recording Precision 94%
Core Concept 3

Operational Definitions

Translating abstract concepts (like "customer confusion") into explicit, observable physical indicators (like "frowning, pausing over 10 seconds, rereading shelf tag").

Construct Validity 98%
Core Concept 4

Observer Bias & Ethics

Guarding against subjective interpretation errors, Hawthorne effect (behavior change when watched), and maintaining consumer privacy in public retail spaces.

Ethical Integrity 99%
Core Concept 5

Pilot Testing & Inter-Rater Reliability

Pilot Testing: Trial-running observation sheets in store aisles to refine categories.
Inter-Rater Agreement: Comparing coding results between multiple observers to ensure consistency.
Reliability Standards 95%
